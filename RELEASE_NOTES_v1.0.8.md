## Smart-ToDo 1.0.8

### More recurrence options
- Repeat dated tasks daily, on weekdays, weekly, or monthly.
- Monthly tasks scheduled on the 29th, 30th, or 31st move to the last valid day in shorter months.
- Recurring due times are preserved when the next occurrence is created.
- Recurrence labels are available in Arabic and English.

### Upgrade
This APK keeps the existing signing certificate and can be installed over Smart-ToDo 1.0.7.
